About the artist

Beauty tailored to African skin tones and warm weather realities

Founded by a dedicated Nigerian makeup artist, Dekeez Beauty exists to deliver high impact, skin focused beauty. Nigerian weddings are vibrant, emotional, and long. Your makeup must withstand energetic dancing, warm humidity, and countless high resolution cameras without separating or changing color.

Every look begins with targeted skin preparation using clean, premium hydration products that balance oils before a single brush touch. Why brides and event celebrants trust Dekeez Beauty

Every service is engineered around longevity, photo fidelity, and skin comfort.

Sweat and humidity resistance

Custom priming layers seal hydration inside while repelling moisture from outside. Your base remains intact through outdoor photography, church tears, and high energy reception dancing.

Result: 16 hours of fresh complexion without mid day caking

Zero flashback camera precision

We use micro milled silica free powders tested under high power camera strobes. You get pure skin glow without white casts, grey undertones, or harsh flashback in professional 4K footage.

Result: Picture perfect portraits from mobile video to wedding album

Punctual call time execution

We map timing down to 45 minutes per bridal party attendant and 90 minutes for the bride. The bridal suite stays peaceful, you finish on schedule, and your wedding photographer never waits.

Result: Zero morning panic and zero rushed moments

Hospital grade sanitation protocol

Single use applicators for mascara and lips, 70 percent isopropyl sanitized brush sets for every face, and stainless steel palette decanting protect sensitive and acne prone skin.

Result: Clean pores, zero post event irritation, total peace of mind
