Terms of service

Deposit and reservation

A 50 percent non refundable deposit is required to reserve any date on the Dekeez Beauty calendar. Date holds are not confirmed until funds clear. The outstanding balance is due 48 hours prior to call time.

Punctuality and call times

Clients and bridal party members must be seated at scheduled call times with clean, moisturized skin. Delays caused by bridal party members exceeding 30 minutes may incur late waiting fees or require look adjustments to safeguard wedding ceremony start times.

Travel and logistics

For destinations outside central Lagos, verified transportation logistics or round trip flight tickets and secure hotel accommodations must be finalized at least two weeks before the event date.
